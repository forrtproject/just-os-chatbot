How the numbers are calculated

Answer models (one row per question in eval/results/run_*.csv)

Runs, Newest run
How many times the model was evaluated, and the date and time (YYMMDD-HHMM) of the run shown.
n
Number of questions in that run.
Correct
Share of questions where the bot did the right thing: it answered a question marked "answer", or refused one marked "refuse". Errors count as wrong.
Answered
Questions marked "answer" that got an answer / all questions marked "answer". The rest were refused or got "no sources".
Refused
Questions marked "refuse" that the bot refused / all questions marked "refuse".
Errors
Questions where a model call failed (time-out, provider error).
Kind
full pipeline: the whole bot, topic check included. papers / FORRT pages: every question sent that way without a topic check (--route), the input of the pair table. In those runs Correct only means "answered", so compare them by judge, citations and hit.
Pair table
For a topic check and an answer model: per question the topic check's decision from the classify run; "neither" counts as refused, "forrt" takes the model's FORRT-pages answer, "open_science" and "both" its papers answer. Correct, hit, judge and citations are then counted as for a full run; seconds and cost add the topic check's.
hit@7
For questions with known source papers (expected_dois): share where at least one of them was among the 7 sources the answer model saw. Only a few questions have them.
Citations valid
Share of answers whose every [n] points to a source that exists (no invented numbers).
Judge
Mean score of eval/judge.py: does the cited passage support the sentence? "–" when the run was not judged.
Rubric coverage
Mean score of eval/rubric_judge.py over the questions with a rubric (the FORRT glossary items): how much of the rubric the answer covers, from 0 to 1. "–" when the run was not judged.
Mean s, p95 s
Seconds from the question to the complete answer: the mean, and the time 95% of questions stay under.
Topic check s, Search s, Re-rank s, LLM answer s
Mean seconds of each step, measured inside the bot: the topic check (router or classifier), the vector and keyword search, the re-ranker that picks the best passages, and the answer model writing the answer (with its one retry). Refused questions stop after the topic check, so the later steps average over the questions that reached them. Questions answered from forrt.org instead of the papers record forrt_pages_s (page search, live fetch and answer) in the logs. "–" for runs from before 12 Nov. The chat log (data/chats/chats.jsonl) and the arena logs record the same steps for every real question.
Tokens / q
Mean prompt + completion tokens per question, all model calls together (topic check, answer, retry).
USD / 1k q
Mean cost per question as reported by OpenRouter, times 1,000.

Topic check (eval/results/classify_*.csv)

Accuracy
Share of questions where the predicted label equals the gold label (route in eval/questions.jsonl; FORRT-only question files count as "forrt").
False refusals
In-scope questions (open science, FORRT or both) that the check turned away (predicted off topic / neither).
False answers
Off-topic questions that the check let through.
Errors
No label at all (the call failed and nothing could fall back).
Mean s, USD / 1k q
Time and cost of the topic check alone per question.

Embedding models

hit@5, hit@20
Share of questions whose source document is in the first 5 / 20 search results.
MRR@20
Mean of 1 / rank of the source document within the first 20 (0 when it is not there): rewards finding it near the top.
ms / query, Dims
Time to embed one question; length of one vector.

Arena

Correct, References, Clarity
Mean rating from 1 (bad) to 5 (very good) that people gave each answer.
Overall
Mean of the three criteria. Mean s: time to the answer.
